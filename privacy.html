Privacy policy

Effective October 17, 2026

House Glover NAS Sync is a personal synchronization application operated by Stefon Glover.

Google data access

The application uses Google Drive authorization to list and read file metadata and contents, and to create, update, rename, and recoverably delete files when carrying out synchronization. Authorization may permit access across the owner's Drive; configured synchronization jobs are limited to designated folders.

Storage and use

File contents, synchronization state, and operational logs are stored on the owner's NAS and in the owner's Google Drive as configured. Credentials are stored in protected configuration files on owner-controlled systems. The owner's workstation may temporarily hold credentials during authorization and setup.

Google data is used only for file synchronization, verification, conflict recovery, and troubleshooting. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models. This informational website contains no Google Drive data and uses no analytics or advertising.

Sharing and control

The application communicates with Google services to perform the requested synchronization. It does not intentionally disclose file contents to other parties. The owner can stop the synchronization jobs, revoke the application's access in Google Account settings, and remove local credentials, logs, and retained backups.
